The Anatomy of a Better Burger

We spent over two years perfecting our smash technique. High heat, heavy press, and pure premium ingredients ensure that signature lace crust on every bite.

  • 01

    Prime Heritage Angus Blend

    A custom 80/20 ratio of brisket, short rib, and chuck ground fresh twice daily. Never frozen, never pre-formed.

  • 02

    Slow-Fermented Brioche

    Light as air yet sturdy enough to hold every drop of juice. Toasted on the butter top until crisp and fragrant.

  • 03

    Small-Batch Artisan Sauces

    Crafted from scratch every morning: smoky bacon jams, charred pepper cremas, and 12-spice golden smash drizzle.

Our Heritage

Born in a Food Truck. Perfected for the Neighborhood.

Sizzle & Bun started with one cast iron griddle, two spatulas, and an obsession with the crispy, caramelized lace edge of a genuine smash burger. Today, we still make every burger to order with the exact same passion.

“The best burger in the city isn’t the tallest one — it’s the one with the crispiest crust and the most flavorful sear.”
— Chef Mateo Brooks, Founder
